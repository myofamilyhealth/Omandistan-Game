👑 Welcome to Omandistan 👸

You serve Mr. & Mrs. Omand, the king and queen who approve every action. Each new building costs 1 💞 Spreading Love token — earn tokens by building Kindness Centers and helping struggling neighbours.

Your mission: shape policy and grow a thriving utopia the size of Alaska. The happier your country, the more people immigrate to it.

  • 🌾 Farms = perfect competition (price-takers)
  • 🏥 Healthcare = monopoly (high price, fewer served)
  • 🛒 Goods = elastic luxury · 🌾🏥 food & care = inelastic necessities

Start by building Housing 🏠 to attract your first settlers.
