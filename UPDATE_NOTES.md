# 👑 Omandistan — Update Notes

Play here: **https://myofamilyhealth.github.io/Omandistan-Game/**
(If something looks old, hard-refresh once: `Ctrl+Shift+R` / `Cmd+Shift+R`.)

---

## v10 — Quality of Life 🔁 *(latest)*

- **Auto-accept kindness aid.** Tired of the 💞 popups pausing your game? Click
  "🔁 Always accept automatically" inside any kindness request, or flip the new
  **Kindness Aid** toggle in Royal Decrees. Requests still arrive at the same rate
  with the same costs and rewards — they just resolve instantly in the log without
  stopping time. (Skipped safely if you can't afford one.)
- 📣 **New game link!** The creator's GitHub username changed, so the game moved to
  **myofamilyhealth.github.io/Omandistan-Game** — update your bookmarks; the old
  link is dead.

## v9 — City Living 🏢

- **Five tiers of housing**, each holding different numbers of people:
  - 🏚️ **Low-End Housing** — 10 people, dirt cheap, zero charm
  - 🏠 **House** — 8 people, the classic
  - 🏢 **Apartments** — 24 people in one dense tower
  - 🏙️ **Condos** — 14 people + a happiness bonus
  - 🏘️ **High-End Villas** — 6 wealthy residents, big happiness boost
- **Organized build menu.** The toolbar is now collapsible categories
  (Residential, Industry & Tech, Water & Power, Civic & Fun…) — no more one giant
  list. Buy Land / Upgrade / Bulldoze stay pinned on top.
- **New tech industry:** 💾 **Chip Fab**, 🖥️ **Data Center** (huge value, drinks
  electricity), 🤖 **Robotics Lab**.
- **Realism pass.** Buildings redrawn with window grids, balconies, pools, roof AC
  units and server lights. **Upgrades now physically grow the building** — extra
  floors, wings, garages, penthouses — and the floating ⭐ stars are gone.

## v8 — Money Gets Real 📈

- **Inflation now hits your wallet.** Shortages push prices up month after month,
  and the rising **CPI (price level)** makes *everything* nominally more expensive:
  buildings, upgrades, land, upkeep, trade. Savings lose buying power in an
  inflationary boom — keep supply ahead of demand! (Overproduce and you'll see mild
  deflation instead.) Toolbar prices update live; CPI shows in the Omand Fed panel.
- **Gas is easier to get.** Gas Mines are cheaper ($110), pump **62% more gas**,
  pollute less, and every difficulty starts with more gas. Buildings also need less
  gas to construct.

## v7 — Learn As You Play 🧠

- **Click any stat in the top bar** (Treasury, Happiness, GDP, PPC, Wood…) for a
  plain-English explanation and 2–3 tips to improve it.
- **World map flow:** clicking a country now opens its trade menu directly.
- Fixed invisible Theme Parks / Cinemas / Stadiums (they charged you but didn't
  draw — oops). Added version tags so your browser always loads the newest build.

## v6 — The Inspector & The Grid ⚡

- **🧐 The Guy Who Knows Things** (a.k.a. **Alan Tu**) visits once a year and
  inspects your country on happiness, economy, calm, utilities, clean air and
  services. Score **82+** and he invests a fortune. It's meant to be hard.
- **Water & power systems.** 🚰 Water Plants pipe water underground; generate
  electricity with ⚡ Power Plants, ☀️ Solar, 🌬️ Wind, ☢️ Nuclear, plus 🗼 Power
  Lines. **Brownouts cut production** — keep the lights on.
- 💧 Water and ⚡ Power joined the demand bars.

## v5 — Choose Your Challenge 🎚️

- **Difficulty levels** at game start: 🌱 Easy / ⚖️ Normal / 🔥 Hard — sets starting
  money & resources, income, upkeep, pollution severity and immigration speed.
- Fixed the layout bug where side panels covered the map and hid the demand bars.

## v4 — The People Speak 😠

- **📊 Citizen demand bars** (bottom-right): live supply-vs-demand for housing,
  food, goods, healthcare, fun and resources. Red = build more; blue = surplus
  sitting idle. Empty houses attract nobody!
- **The Omands are the Fed.** Countercyclical fiscal policy: taxes rise in booms
  and fall in slumps (toggle to manual in 🏛️ Fiscal Policy). A **money vault**
  appeared beside the castle.
- **Pollution** from heavy industry lowers happiness and smogs the map — Parks 🌳
  clean it. **Unrest** builds from taxes, pollution, joblessness and shortages, and
  angry citizens **protest**.
- **Fun buildings:** 🎬 Cinema, 🏟️ Stadium, 🎢 Theme Park.
- Renamed nations: gas → **CastanoControls**, tech → **GooseGoods** 🪿.

## v3 — Upgrades & Inspection ⬆️

- **Every building is upgradable** (★→★★→★★★): more output, capacity and jobs,
  with bigger graphics at each level.
- **Click any building** to inspect it — level, exact stats, and an Upgrade button
  with the precise cost.

## v2 — The Wider World 🌍

- **Cars drive on your roads**, and every building must touch a road.
- **Buy districts** to expand your island across a bigger sea.
- **Resources:** 🪵 Wood, 🛢️ Oil, 🔥 Gas — extracted by Lumber Camps, Oil Derricks
  and Gas Mines; construction costs resources, not just money.
- **GDP = C + I + G + Xn** with real business sectors feeding each component.
- **Four island nations** to trade with — Elliott Emperace (oil), Wardmania (wood),
  and friends. They only accept deals that **expand their own PPC**. Set **tariffs**
  (revenue, but soured relations). Trade unlocks when you build a 🚢 Seaport.
- World map with fast travel + a minimap.

## v1 — The Founding 👑

- Isometric city-builder with Mr. & Mrs. Omand approving every build for
  **1 💞 Spreading Love token** — earned through Kindness Centers and helping
  struggling neighbours.
- AP Econ core: 🌾 farms in **perfect competition**, 🏥 healthcare **monopoly**

  human capital → **PPC**, GDP, unemployment, inflation, and immigration that grows
  with happiness.
- The Omands' castle offshore, interactive tutorial, acts-of-kindness events.
